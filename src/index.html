About

Hi! I'm Emir Baha Yıldırım (aka Jay Levin for easier pronunciation). I'm a sophomore civil engineering student and undergraduate researcher at Middle East Technical University (METU), while also aiming for a career as a systems developer. I've been programming on and off for the past 12 years—and diving deep into low-level systems for the past three. When I'm not studying structural responses or writing C, I'm a TTRPG, aviation, and gaming nerd, and I love talking about all three.
